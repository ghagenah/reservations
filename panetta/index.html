Event checklist

100 Panetta Conference is a NO-HOST event space. Set-up and clean-up is the responsibility of the Responsible Party.

I agree to all of the following conditions:

  1. Begin and end your event on time (reservation times include setup and cleanup).
  2. Return the facility, tables and chairs to a neat configuration (2 chairs per table). Any extra chairs used should be returned to the chair dolly.
  3. Wipe down tables, chairs and lectern using provided sanitary wipes (including on patios).
  4. Wipe clean and return extra chairs to the chair rack in the chair room.
  5. Remove and tie trash bags. Place them into the provided large white trash can in the chair room.
  6. Close and lock all windows and balcony doors. Leave the balcony key in the lectern!
  7. Turn off all A/V equipment, including D-Ten. Projector and screen are controlled from the wall controller. All other AV equipment is controlled from the AV cart MAIN ON/OFF switch.
  8. Turn off conference room lights (hall & stair lights are on motion sensors).
  9. Close conference room doors.
  10. Return the 4-wheel cart to the 3rd floor chair room.

Updated 8/2026

I agree that if the above items are not completed, and 100 Panetta staff time is required to complete any of these tasks, our department will cover the costs of returning the room to its original condition.

If items are damaged or missing, our department agrees to repair or replace items.

We agree to provide a department FOAPAL should there be any charges related to the facility condition after our event.

We acknowledge that this reservation is for the 3rd floor conference room only. Access to other parts of the building is not included in this reservation, other than the additional rest room on the second floor, unless otherwise arranged separately.
